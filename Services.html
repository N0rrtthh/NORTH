Services

I offer various kinds of services that involve digital media

2D Animation
2D Animation

Widely used for animated movies, cartoons, marketing videos, advertisements, corporate presentations, educational materials, and video games.

3D Animation
3D Animation

A graphic technique that uses motion to bring characters, objects, and props to life — used in video games, films, TV shows, and more.

Claymation
Claymation

A form of stop-motion animation where each piece is made of a malleable substance, usually plasticine clay, making it fully deformable.

Modelling
Modelling

Creating three-dimensional representations of objects using specialized software — conveying size, shape, and texture with precision.

Cut-out Animation
Cut-out Animation

A form of stop-motion using flat characters and backgrounds cut from paper, card, or photographs, used as puppets for stop motion.

Editing
Editing

Selecting and preparing visual, audible, or cinematic material to convey a message — from rough cuts to polished final deliverables.

VFX
VFX

Combining CGI with live-action footage to create scenes that wouldn't otherwise be possible — used in almost every type of film and TV production.

Animatics
Animatics

Defining timing for a piece of moving image. When used with a soundtrack, they're a quick way to get a sense of a finished piece.

Rotoscoping
Rotoscoping

Creating animated sequences by tracing over live-action footage frame by frame — producing lifelike characters that move like real people.

Kinetic Typography
Kinetic Typography

An animation technique that uses moving text to capture attention, set a tone, and entertain audiences.

Compositing
Compositing

Combining multiple layers of images or video elements to render a final still or moving image — physical or software-based.

SFX
SFX

Artificially created or enhanced sounds used to emphasize artistic content in films, TV shows, animation, video games, and music.
