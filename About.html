About Me

— Who am I? —

Elroni F. Quiñones

Hi! My name is Elroni F. Quiñones and I am an animator. I had been interested in animation mainly due to the fact that I'm an avid anime fan. Animators play a crucial part in developing excellent visual content in different media. To flourish in this role I pursued and studied animation, focusing on learning the tools and techniques an animator should know. I am known by my pen name NORTH, under which I have established a business. Animation work calls for audio/video editing and knowledge of cutting-edge software like Adobe Flash and 3D Studio Max, but it also encompasses soft skills such as attention to detail, patience, and communication.


This portfolio contains my training experience — the works I completed (Portfolio section), the skills I offer (Services section), and information on how to reach me (Contact section).
